Implements §5 of proposals/caret-and-selection.md. For a multi-line selection L1's right edge extends to content_right because the selection wraps past this line — so L1, L2, and middle lines share a tidy right edge instead of a ragged one. A non-wrapping single-line selection keeps the natural inline behaviour and hugs the last glyph on both sides. In this mock that's enforced with a flex L1 line where the .sel span has flex:1 and grows to the line's right edge; middle lines paint the line div itself; trailing lines wrap the head of the line in an inline-block .sel span.
