Implements §4, §6–§8 of proposals/caret-and-selection.md. Selection fill is drawn under the text pass; selected glyphs are overdrawn in a second pass clipped to the selection range. The disabled overlay (× 0.5 alpha) is applied last, so a disabled widget that holds a selection paints as if the selection weren't there — the range is preserved in selection_anchor but not rendered.
