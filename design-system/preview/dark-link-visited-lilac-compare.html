Charoite sits between ArtyClick’s saturated Lilac and the desaturated Figma variant. The pale color-name.com hex is too close to HighlightedText (#FFFFFF) to be distinguishable, especially on Eclipse.
* coined name — sister to Quartzite; the mineral is a real lilac-purple silicate (K(Ca,Na)2Si4O10(OH,F)·H2O) found in Russia.
